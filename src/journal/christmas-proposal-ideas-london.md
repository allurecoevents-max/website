## 1. A candlelit hotel suite

Completely private, warm and impossible to rain on. Picture a room filled with candles, roses and a quiet glow from the city outside, with champagne waiting. A festive weekend away is an easy cover story.

## 2. A winter rooftop with blankets and lights

A rooftop terrace with fairy lights, heaters, faux-fur throws and a view across London feels magical in December. Book a private or semi-private space, and always have an indoor option ready in case of wind or rain.

## 3. Under the Christmas tree at home

Sometimes the most meaningful place is your own living room. We transform it while you're out, with candles, flowers, a personalised sign and the tree glowing behind you. Walk in together and ask. It's intimate, relaxed and you're not watching the clock.

## 4. A private dining room

Dinner is the most natural cover story there is in December. A private room with a styled table, a floral arch or a "Marry me?" sign means you can ask before the first course, or with dessert, and celebrate straight away.

## 5. A festive evening walk that ends somewhere special

Start with a stroll through the Christmas lights, a hot chocolate and a market, then end the evening at a setup you've had prepared nearby. The walk relaxes you both, and the surprise feels effortless.

## 6. New Year's Eve, just the two of you

Countdown proposals are popular, but crowds make them hard to photograph and hard to hear. A private room or terrace where you can see the fireworks, with just the two of you, gives you the moment without the crush.

## Tips for a festive proposal

- **Book early.** December evenings are the busiest of the year for restaurants, hotels and photographers.
- **Pick your timing carefully.** Christmas Day itself can be emotional and busy with family. Many people prefer the days just before, or New Year's Eve.
- **Plan for the weather.** London winters are cold, dark early and often wet. Have a covered or indoor plan B for anything outside.
- **Think about the light.** It gets dark around 4pm in December, so outdoor golden-hour photos mean an early afternoon proposal. Candles and fairy lights look beautiful after dark instead.
- **Keep the secret.** Shared calendars, bank notifications and excited friends spoil more surprises than anything else.
- **Plan what happens after.** Family waiting at home, a dinner booking, or a night away: decide in advance so the moment flows into a celebration.
