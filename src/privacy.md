Last updated: November 2026

Allure & Co Events ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it.

## What we collect

When you fill in our enquiry or checklist form we collect the details you give us: your name, email address, phone number and information about your event (type, date, guest count, budget and your ideas).

## How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your enquiry, provide our services, send you the checklist you requested, and, if you become a client, plan your event. We don't sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

## Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree, a form-handling service. Our website is hosted by Netlify. These providers process data on our behalf.

## Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address to deliver them. If you book a call through our Calendly booking calendar, Calendly processes your booking details and may set cookies needed for the calendar to work.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 2 years, and client records for as long as required for legal and accounting purposes.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email us at [allurecoevents@gmail.com](mailto:allurecoevents@gmail.com). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
