## You only tell your story once

A good proposal starts with questions: how you met, what your partner loves, the places that mean something to you both. By the end of planning it, your planner knows your style, your families and the little details that make you *you*.

When that same planner moves on to your wedding, none of it is lost. Your first wedding meeting starts from what they already know, not from a blank form.

## It feels like one story, not separate events

The proposal, the engagement party, the bridal shower and the wedding can each look completely different, but when one person plans them all, they connect. A flower from the proposal can return in the bouquet. The colours from the engagement party can carry into the wedding stationery. Guests notice, even if they can't say why.

## Fewer people to brief, fewer things to go wrong

Every new planner means another round of calls, another contract and another person learning which suppliers you like. One planner already has your shortlist: the florist you loved, the photographer who captured the proposal, the venue that felt right. That saves time, and usually money too.

## Someone who already knows how you work

Do you like to see three options or ten? Do you want a weekly update or only the big decisions? After a proposal together, your planner knows. Wedding planning is long, and working with someone who already understands how you make decisions makes it much calmer.

## What to ask before you book

Whoever you choose, ask these questions at the first call:

- **Do you plan proposals and weddings yourself, or pass one of them on?**
- **Will I work with the same person the whole way through?**

- **How do you keep track of what we decided at the proposal stage?**

## Where to start

You don't need to book everything at once. Start with the moment in front of you, whether that's a surprise proposal at Christmas, an engagement party in the spring or a wedding next year, and see how it feels to work together. If it feels right, we'll be there for the next chapter too.
