faq:

  heading: Good to know
  items:
    - question: How small is a micro wedding?
      answer: Usually up to about 40 guests, and sometimes just the two of you and two witnesses. We plan anything from an elopement-style ceremony to a long lunch for 40.
    - question: How far in advance do we need to book the registry office?
      answer: Popular London register offices can book up months ahead, especially on Fridays and Saturdays. In England and Wales you also need to give notice of marriage at your local register office at least 29 days before the ceremony. We'll help you plan the timings.
    - question: Can we have the ceremony at home or in a garden?
      answer: Right now, a legal ceremony in England and Wales has to take place in a register office or an approved venue. Many couples have a short legal ceremony first, then a symbolic ceremony and celebration wherever they like. The Government plans to change the law so legal weddings can happen in more places, but it isn't law yet.
    - question: Is a weekday wedding cheaper?
      answer: Often, yes. Many venues and suppliers have lower prices from Monday to Thursday, and you'll usually have more choice of dates.
