## Why book early

Restaurants, hotels, florists and photographers are at their busiest around 14 February. Many popular places fill weeks in advance, and roses cost more that week than any other. Planning early means you get the setting, the flowers and the photographer you actually want.

## 1. A private room, not a busy restaurant

A crowded restaurant on Valentine's night is noisy and public, and the next table is probably proposing too. A private dining room, or a restaurant booked for an early or late sitting, gives you the same romance with none of the audience.

## 2. A candlelit suite with a city view

Book a hotel suite for the evening and have it transformed before you arrive: candles, roses, a floral arch or a "Marry me?" sign, and champagne on ice. Completely private, and a night away is the perfect cover story.

## 3. A rooftop under the stars

Fairy lights, heaters, blankets and London glittering below. February is cold, so choose a covered or heated terrace, and always have an indoor plan B.

## 4. Recreate your first date

Go back to where it all started: the restaurant, the bar or the walk from your first date. Add a few surprises along the way, then end the evening at a styled setup nearby. It's personal in a way no venue can be.

## 5. At home, transformed

Come home to a living room you don't recognise: hundreds of candles, petals, flowers and a personalised sign. Relaxed, private and you can celebrate in your pyjamas afterwards.

## 6. Propose on a different day

If you want the romance without the crowds, propose the weekend before or after. You get better availability, calmer venues, and an anniversary that's all your own.

## Tips for a Valentine's proposal

- **Book your photographer first.** They're in high demand that week, and the photos are what you'll keep forever.
- **Expect them to suspect something.** A Valentine's dinner is a classic cover story. Add a twist, like a "dinner with friends" or a surprise earlier in the day.
- **Plan for the dark and the cold.** It's dark by early evening in February, so candles and fairy lights will do the work.
- **Make it about them.** Their favourite flowers, song or place matters more than any trend.
