## Set your budget first

Before you look at a single venue, decide on one number you're comfortable with, and write down everything it needs to cover: the setting, flowers, candles, a photographer, food or drinks afterwards. Leave a little aside for the unexpected. Knowing your limit makes every other decision easier.

## Avoid the 14 February premium

Restaurants, hotels and florists are at their busiest on Valentine's Day itself, and some charge more for it. Proposing on the 13th, the weekend before or the weekend after can feel just as romantic, with more choice and calmer suppliers. Your partner will still know exactly why you chose that week.

## Where it's worth spending

- **A photographer.** Even half an hour of a professional hiding nearby gives you photos you'll keep forever. If you only spend on one thing, make it this.
- **One statement piece.** A floral heart, an arch of roses or a "Marry me?" sign becomes the centre of every photo. Spend on one strong focal point, not lots of small things.
- **Candlelight.** Lots of candles cost relatively little and transform any room. Use real candles only where the venue allows them, and LED ones everywhere else.

## Where you can save

- **Propose at home.** A living room transformed with candles, petals and flowers is personal, private and free of venue fees.
- **Use a place that's free to visit.** A favourite park, the spot where you first met or a quiet garden at golden hour. Check the rules on decorations and permits first.
- **Seasonal flowers.** Roses are at their most expensive in February. Seasonal flowers like tulips, ranunculus and anemones look beautiful and often cost less.
- **Dinner afterwards at home.** Book a private chef or order from your favourite restaurant instead of a fixed Valentine's menu.

## Simple ideas that still feel special

1. **A candlelit room at home**, waiting for them after an evening out.
2. **A sunset proposal** in the place your story started.
3. **A picnic proposal** with blankets, flowers and a hidden photographer, weather permitting, with an indoor backup.
4. **A memory trail**: notes or photos leading through your home to the moment itself.
5. **A rooftop at golden hour**, with one beautiful floral piece and nothing else.

## Plan early

Whatever your budget, the earlier you plan, the more you save. Four to six weeks ahead gives you the best choice of photographers and flowers, and time to compare prices. Last-minute bookings around Valentine's Day almost always cost more.
